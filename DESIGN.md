# ClearLedger design direction

Approved direction: bright, calm ledger with deep olive navigation, clear tables, and warm chart colors.

## Tokens

- Canvas: #F5F6F2; panels: #FFFFFF; text: #253126; secondary text: #626B60.
- Navigation: #233326; accent: #BFE47B; active navigation: #3B4C32.
- Charts: olive #6C863E, sage #B6C68F, terracotta #CB916C, ochre #D5B568.
- Typography: a clean humanist sans; tabular financial numerals.
- Spacing: 4px base, 24–32px section gaps, 12–16px panel radii, thin neutral dividers.

## Surface

Mode: operate. The main job is to understand this month's spending and quickly record a transaction. A desktop sidebar becomes compact navigation on mobile. Sample mode is explicitly labeled. Primary controls use olive or lime with strong contrast. Charts support exact text values and do not replace them.

## Interaction

Fast entry, visible labels and keyboard focus, calm hover transitions, and reduced-motion support. Empty, invalid, over-budget, and storage-error states stay within the same visual system.
